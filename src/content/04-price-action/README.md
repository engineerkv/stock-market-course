# Step 4: Price Action

Price action means reading what price itself is doing, candle by candle, at the levels that matter. In Step 2 you learned to name candles. In Step 3 you learned larger shapes. This step puts them together into complete setups, then adds a few tools that confirm what price is already telling you.

The market is a tug of war between buyers and sellers. Candles show who is winning at each moment. Your job is to trade with the side that is winning, at a price where you can prove yourself wrong cheaply. Before every trade, ask yourself: "Why am I in this trade?" The answer should always come from price action.
