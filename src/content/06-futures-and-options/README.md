# Step 6: Futures and Options

Steps 1 to 5 teach you how to form a view: which way price should go, how far, and where you are wrong. This step shows how to express that view with futures and options. A good choice of instrument can lower the money you put at risk, protect a position you already hold, or earn income in a sideways market.

**A warning first.** Derivatives use leverage. Leverage makes gains bigger, and losses bigger too. A study by India's market regulator, SEBI, found that about 9 in 10 individual traders in equity futures and options lost money in the 2022 financial year. Do not trade derivatives until you can follow the risk rules in [Step 1, Lesson 2](../01-foundations/02-method-risk-and-mindset.md) without exception.

**Contract details change.** Lot sizes, expiry days, margins, and tick sizes are set by each exchange and are revised often. The numbers in this step are made up to explain the ideas. Always check the exchange's current contract specifications and expiry calendar before you trade.
